    Commercial-grade treadmills with speeds up to 20 km/h, incline up to 15%, and built-in heart rate monitoring. Perfect for warm-ups, steady-state cardio, and interval training.

    Cardio Fat Burn Endurance

    Bench Press

    Olympic flat benches with safety spotter arms and adjustable incline/decline positions. Loaded with premium barbells and weight plates for maximum chest and tricep development.

    Chest Triceps Strength
    🔱

    Squat Rack

    Heavy-duty power racks rated to 500 kg with adjustable J-hooks, safety bars, and pull-up attachments. Ideal for squats, deadlifts, overhead press, and rack pulls.
